SECTION 1: COMPANY OVERVIEW
============================================================

Reps & Revenue is a website design and development agency for small businesses. We build professional, mobile-ready websites for tradespeople, coaches, consultants, creatives, health and fitness professionals, and other service-based businesses across the UK.

The business was founded by three professionals-a digital marketer, a developer, and a lawyer-who combined their expertise to give small businesses access to high-quality web design without the agency price tag or long wait times.

Reps & Revenue acts as a growth partner, helping clients establish a strong online presence that turns visitors into customers.

SECTION 3: SERVICE OFFERINGS
============================================================

Reps & Revenue's primary service is website design and development for small businesses.

### 3.1 Website Design & Build
- Custom, mobile-responsive websites tailored to the client's brand and goals.
- Up to 5 core pages typically included (e.g. Home, About, Services, Contact, Gallery/Testimonials).
- Contact forms, social media links, and basic SEO setup.
- Built for speed, clarity, and conversion.

### 3.2 Pricing
- **£50 deposit** to start the build. Non-refundable.
- **£250 completion fee** if the client loves the site and wants to keep it.
- The £250 includes:
  - Full website ownership.
  - One year of hosting.
  - Unlimited future assistance, maintenance, and updates (for as long as Reps & Revenue hosts the site).
- Unlimited revisions are included during the build at no extra cost, provided they are reasonable and within scope (decided at the team's discretion).

### 3.3 Process
1. **Sales call** - Walk through templates and examples, explain pricing, and close on the £50 deposit.
2. **Onboarding** - Gather branding, colours, layout, fonts, images, and any specific requirements.
3. **Design & build** - First draft delivered within 4-7 days (usually sooner).
4. **Revisions** - Unlimited revisions at no extra cost until the client is happy.
5. **Launch** - Pay the £250 completion fee and the site goes live.

### 3.4 Bespoke Add-Ons & Custom Solutions
For clients with bigger plans, Reps & Revenue offers:
- CRM integration.
- Conversational chatbots.
- Advanced automations and tooling.
- Booking, membership, or e-commerce functionality.
- Multi-site or white-label solutions.
- Contact the sales team for a custom quote.

### 3.5 Flexible Plans
- Clients on a budget can speak to the sales team about flexible payment plans.

SECTION 11: FREQUENTLY USED TERMS & CONTEXT
============================================================

- **Deposit**: The £50 non-refundable payment that starts a website build.
- **Completion fee**: The £250 payment due when the client approves the site and wants it to go live.
- **Hosting**: Storage and serving of the website files so it is accessible online.
- **Revisions**: Changes made to the design or content during the build process.
- **Mobile responsive**: A design that works well on phones, tablets, and desktops.
- **Bespoke add-ons**: Custom features such as chatbots, CRM integration, or booking systems.
- **Onboarding form**: The form clients complete after paying the deposit to share branding, content, and design preferences.
