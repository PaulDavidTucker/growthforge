\section{How Our Workflow Works}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Onboarding call} - We confirm what you need, the style you like and any specific features.
    \item \textbf{Deposit} - We take the £50 deposit and add your build to our queue.
    \item \textbf{Templates and ideas} - We show you templates and live examples to narrow down what you want.
    \item \textbf{We deliver what you want} - Your site is built to match your brand, content and goals.
    \item \textbf{Any revisions} - We make unlimited revisions (within reason) until you are happy.
    \item \textbf{Full payment} - If you want the site live, the £250 completion fee covers ownership, hosting for one year and ongoing support.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Things to Think About}

To help us build the perfect site, please gather your thoughts and assets on the following.

\subsection{Branding}
Do you have an existing logo, brand guidelines or a particular feeling you want the site to convey? Examples: modern and clean, bold and energetic, calm and premium, local and trustworthy.

\subsection{Colours}
What colours represent your brand? If you do not have exact hex codes, share examples of websites, images or palettes you like.

\subsection{Layout}
What pages do you need? Common examples:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item Home, About, Services, Contact
    \item Home, About, Classes / Booking, Contact
    \item Home, Portfolio, Services, Testimonials, Contact
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Fonts}
Do you have a preferred font style? Examples: classic serif, clean sans-serif, bold display font, handwritten feel.

\subsection{Images}
Good photos make a huge difference. Please provide:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item Logo files (PNG, SVG or PDF)
    \item Photos of your work, team or premises
    \item Any icons, graphics or videos you want included
\end{itemize}
