\section{The Offer}

Reps \& Revenue builds professional, mobile-ready websites for small businesses.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{£50 deposit} to start the build (non-refundable).
    \item We deliver a first version within \textbf{4-7 days}.
    \item \textbf{Unlimited revisions} are included at no extra cost while we refine the design (within reason, at the team's discretion).
    \item If you love the site and want to keep it, a further \textbf{£250} covers:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Full website ownership
        \item One year of hosting
        \item Unlimited future assistance, maintenance and updates (for as long as we host the site)
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

No hidden fees, no surprise invoices - just a simple, transparent process.

\section{How the Sales Call Works}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Introduction} --- confirm the prospect's business, goals and current online presence.
    \item \textbf{Show the examples} above (and any templates) to demonstrate our style and quality.
    \item \textbf{Explain the process} --- discovery, deposit, build, revisions, final payment.
    \item \textbf{Close on the £50 deposit} to secure their build slot.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Deposit and Payment Terms}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item The \textbf{£50 deposit is non-refundable}. It covers the initial design and build work.
    \item We include unlimited revisions at no extra cost while the site is being developed, provided they are reasonable and within scope.
    \item The \textbf{£250 completion fee} is due when the client confirms they want to keep the site and go live.
    \item The completion fee includes one year of hosting, maintenance, updates and support.
\end{itemize}
