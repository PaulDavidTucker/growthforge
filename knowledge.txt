SECTION 1: COMPANY OVERVIEW
============================================================

Reps & Revenue is a digital agency that specializes in helping fitness-based businesses attract, convert, and retain high-value clients. The company integrates marketing strategy, automation, and technology to accelerate growth for gyms, personal trainers, fitness studios, and health brands.

The agency was founded by three passionate professionals—a digital marketer, a developer, and a lawyer—who combined their expertise to create a holistic solution for small businesses. Their approach blends technical precision, legal credibility, and creative marketing to generate measurable revenue impact.

Reps & Revenue operates as a growth partner rather than a vendor, embedding themselves into client businesses to streamline sales funnels, automate client acquisition, and deliver consistent new member growth.

SECTION 3: SERVICE OFFERINGS
============================================================

Reps & Revenue provides a suite of marketing and technology services designed around the sales funnel lifecycle. Each service directly contributes to the process of attracting leads, converting them into clients, and nurturing them into long-term members.

### 3.1 Ad Campaigns & Marketing
- Design and execution of advertising campaigns across social platforms such as Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google Ads, and YouTube.
- Creation of performance-driven ad packages tailored to the fitness niche.
- Focus on ROI and lead quality over vanity metrics.

### 3.2 Website & Funnel Development
- End-to-end design and development of high-converting websites and sales funnels.
- Optimization of user experience (UX) for mobile-first audiences.
- Integration with analytics tools and CRM systems.
- Branded landing pages optimized for conversions, appointment bookings, or program sign-ups.

### 3.3 Chatbots & Automation
- Custom chatbot development to help businesses automate client inquiries and bookings.
- Integration of AI-driven automation pipelines for lead capture, follow-ups, and onboarding.
- Chatbots built using no-code and low-code tools as well as custom logic depending on business size and scope.

### 3.4 Legal & Compliance Support
- Guidance on marketing and data-collection compliance provided by the agency’s in-house legal expertise.
- Ensuring client systems adhere to GDPR and advertising policies.

### 3.5 Consulting & Strategic Support
- One-to-one growth consultations for fitness-based businesses.
- Funnel audits and marketing strategy sessions.
- Long-term optimization support for conversion and retention.

- Most engagements begin with a discovery consultation and data audit.
